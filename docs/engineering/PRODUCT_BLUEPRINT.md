# KingTurf Business OS — Product Engineering Blueprint V1.0

## 1. Objective

Build an artificial-turf industry Business Operating System that connects market acquisition, customer management, technical requirements, product configuration, costing, quotation, credit, contracts, orders, procurement, manufacturing, quality, warehouse, delivery, receivables, collection, commissions, performance, legal evidence and AI-driven management.

The system is judged by business outcomes, not by module completion.

## 2. End-to-End Business Chain

Lead -> Customer -> Opportunity -> CTR -> Technical Solution -> Cost Sheet -> Quote -> Credit -> Contract -> Sales Order -> Production Demand -> MRP/Purchase -> Work Order -> Quality -> Finished Goods -> Shipment -> Receipt -> Invoice -> AR -> Payment -> Commission -> Order P&L -> Close

All core records must be traceable through a Business Order ID.

## 3. Three Experience Surfaces

### Sales Workspace
Mobile-first workspace for sales, export and field personnel: targets, tasks, leads, customers, opportunities, visits, CTR, quotes, contracts, orders, collections, commissions, daily reports, exhibitions, business trips and expenses.

### Operation Workspace
Role-specific work queues for sales operations, technical, purchasing, planning, workshop, quality, warehouse, finance, legal and HR.

### Management Cockpit
Exception-first management cockpit for revenue, margin, cash, AR, overdue exposure, orders, capacity, inventory, quality, risk, people and forecasts.

## 6. Mandatory Rule Engines

- Sales Policy Engine
- Price / CPQ Engine
- Cost Engine
- Credit Engine
- Approval Engine
- Commission Engine
- Risk Engine

Rules must be configurable and versioned. Historical transactions retain the exact policy/rule version applied at decision time.

## 7. Cost Model

Four levels:

1. Standard Cost
2. Quotation Estimated Cost
3. Actual Manufacturing Cost
4. Order Full Cost

Order Full Cost includes actual manufacturing cost plus logistics, commission, rebate, allocated commercial expense, after-sales cost, quality loss, finance cost and bad-debt impact.

Every completed order produces Order P&L and planned-vs-actual cost variance.

## 8. Credit and Shipment Controls

Sales may request payment terms but cannot grant credit independently.

Shipment release must check at minimum:

- valid contract
- required payment received
- credit limit
- current exposure
- overdue status
- quality release
- legal/risk hold

Failed hard gates place the order on hold. Overrides require explicit exception approval and audit evidence.

## 9. Commission Principles

Commission is generated by versioned policy and recorded in a Commission Ledger.

Lifecycle: ACCRUED -> FROZEN -> RELEASED -> PAID, with CLAWED_BACK and CANCELLED where required.

Commission may depend on eligible revenue, margin, collection, quality and risk factors. Uncollected or disputed amounts can freeze related commission.

## 10. Production Boundary

P2 minimum viable MES flow:

Sales Order -> Production Demand -> Production Order -> Work Order -> Material Issue -> Work Reporting -> Batch/Roll -> Quality -> Finished Goods Receipt.

Advanced APS, OEE and PLC/IoT acquisition are later enhancements and must not block the first production-cost loop.

## 11. Evidence and Audit

Core business records use logical deletion and immutable audit history. Sensitive objects require versioning.

Evidence Center must construct an evidence timeline and, for debt recovery, generate a Debt Evidence Package containing customer identity, approvals, quotations, contracts/signatures, orders, production/delivery records, invoices, reconciliation, bank/payment evidence, collection notices and audit records.

## 12. Security Model

RBAC + Data Scope + Field Permission + Action Permission.

Data scopes include SELF, TEAM, DEPARTMENT, REGION, COMPANY and GROUP.

Default-deny for protected actions.

## 13. AI Business Brain

Initial agents:

- Sales Agent
- Credit Agent
- Production Agent
- Cost Agent
- Collection Agent
- Management Agent

Operating loop: Read -> Analyze -> Recommend -> Task -> Track -> Escalate.

AI does not independently approve high-risk price, credit, contract, payment or shipment decisions in the initial releases.

## 15. Engineering Red Lines

1. No formal customer production without an authorized sales/order source.
2. No direct manual overwrite of confirmed payment totals.
3. Sales cannot alter official company collection accounts.
4. Effective historical policy/commission versions cannot be overwritten.
5. Core records cannot be silently deleted.
6. Shipment controls cannot be bypassed without auditable exception approval.

## 16. Acceptance Philosophy

A phase is accepted by running real end-to-end scenarios, not by declaring individual modules complete.

P1 primary acceptance: one real order can progress from customer acquisition to 100% collection and commission calculation without an external Excel ledger, with complete Order 360 traceability and audit history.
